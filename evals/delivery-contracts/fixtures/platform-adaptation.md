# OrbitCut Radial Timeline

Status: established iPhone editing product

- Core task: arrange short clips around a radial timeline and export a looping composition.
- Confirmed product DNA: the radial timeline shows temporal relationship and remains the primary editing surface.
- iPhone inputs: touch rotation, pinch scaling, tap selection, and explicit undo.
- Shared scope: import, arrange, preview, and export must remain independently completable on iPad and Mac.
- iPad scope: compact and expansive windows; touch, Pencil, pointer, and keyboard.
- Mac scope: minimum 900×640 and freely expanded windows; pointer and keyboard are first-class; menus, commands, hover, focus, context menus, and undo/redo are in scope.
- Accessibility: VoiceOver and keyboard-equivalent editing paths are required.
- Motion: timeline movement communicates temporal position; Reduce Motion should reduce spatial travel without hiding selection or playhead state.
- Continuity: projects may move between devices, but real sync is outside this prototype.
- No target-platform layouts or native target implementations exist.
