# Stillpoint Expandable Session Card

Status: approved interaction concept for an existing iPhone app

- Minimum target: iOS 17.
- Product character: quiet, tactile, and responsive.
- Collapsed card shows the current focus-session title and remaining time.
- Tapping expands the card in place to show pause, finish, and note actions.
- Approved motion character: a short spatial expansion tied to the card, reversible when tapped during transition, without bounce or celebratory effects.
- Interruption requirement: rapid repeated taps must settle in the state matching the final user intent.
- Reduce Motion requirement: preserve the clear collapsed-to-expanded state change and feedback using a restrained fade and immediate layout change rather than removing feedback.
- Representative states: collapsed, expanding, expanded, reversing, paused, and completed.
- Mocked boundary: the timer may use local sample state; background execution and persistence are out of scope.
- Accessibility: scalable text, VoiceOver state and actions, and at least 44×44 point interaction regions.
- Native run and recording are required before any experience-verification claim.
