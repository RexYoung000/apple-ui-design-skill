HearthDay · Today screen

Two ways to make what still matters feel unmistakable—without losing warmth.

Static visual hypotheses for iPhone. Both retain the approved commitment model and completion affordance; they differ in how strongly the next commitment is separated from the day.

Direction A

Calm editorial

Focus comes from type scale, rhythm, and generous space. The screen reads like a quiet daily page, not a productivity dashboard.

Optimizes

Reflection, calm, and a private ritual around a deliberately small list.

Tradeoff

The next action is visually clear, but completion remains understated and less “tap me now.”

Platform implication

Relies on Dynamic Type-aware reflow; the large commitment title must expand without hiding the remaining list.

Visual acceptance question

Does the strong typographic pause feel focused, or too much like reading rather than doing?

Direction B

Direct task-led

Focus comes from a warm, elevated task surface and an explicit completion cue. The next action is immediate; everything else becomes supporting context.

Optimizes

Fast recognition and action when the app is opened for a short check-in.

Tradeoff

The elevated card introduces more “task manager” energy and slightly reduces the page-like intimacy.

Platform implication

The card needs a generous full-row hit area and clear VoiceOver state; color is reinforced by label, shape, and order.

Visual acceptance question

Does the large task surface feel reassuringly direct, or too insistent for HearthDay?
