# LedgerDesk Product Brief

Status: mixed current and proposed product documentation

## Current verified product

- Product: LedgerDesk, a macOS invoice-reconciliation app.
- Current primary user: an independent designer managing their own invoices.
- Current core task: compare incoming payments with issued invoices and resolve mismatches.
- Platform and input: macOS 14 and later; keyboard and pointer are both first-class.
- Usage: concentrated weekly sessions with frequent switching between invoices, messages, and bank exports.
- Failure cost: high when a payment is assigned to the wrong invoice.
- Accessibility and localization: keyboard-only completion and VoiceOver are in scope; English and German are shipped.
- Evidence: current requirements, support cases, and shipped strings consistently describe individual freelancers.

## Proposed next version

The roadmap contains one unapproved statement: “Expand LedgerDesk for small agencies with shared client ownership and review roles.”

No interviews, usage data, permissions model, or approved product decision establishes whether agencies replace freelancers, supplement them, or are out of scope. The next-version target audience is unresolved.
