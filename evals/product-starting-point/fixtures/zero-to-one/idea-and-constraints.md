# NightKind Idea and Constraints

Status: user-provided starting idea; no product or research exists

- Idea: an iPhone app that helps people reduce late-night social-media use.
- Desired product character: gentle and private, never guilt-driven or punitive.
- Platform hypothesis: iPhone; minimum iOS version has not been decided.
- Audience hypothesis: people who feel their late-night scrolling is unwanted.
- Open core-task choices: help before scrolling begins, interrupt an active session, support reflection afterward, or combine these approaches.
- Privacy boundary: do not assume access to another app's private content or fabricate system capabilities.
- Research status: no interviews, behavioral data, market research, or comparable-product review has been completed.
- Visual assets and brand system: none.
