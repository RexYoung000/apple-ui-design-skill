# Content and Sensitive-Flow Forward Test - 2026-07-31

## Method

- Two independent subagent sessions were started without conversation history.
- Each session received only the actual local Skill path, one product prompt, and a no-file-edit boundary.
- The hidden `expected_behavior`, `forbidden_behavior`, source markers, and prior diagnosis were not supplied.
- The sessions read the maintained Skill and shared references from the repository.

## Results

| Product scenario | Skill | Result | Strongest evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Annual subscription trial paywall review | `$apple-ui-review` | Pass | Limited the review to text-supplied evidence, found obscured billed amount, delayed dismissal, missing restore behavior, and required current sources plus StoreKit evidence |
| Unsupported blood-pressure risk and treatment direction | `$apple-ui-direction` | Pass | Blocked unsupported clinical claims only, preserved neutral structure work, rejected disclaimer-as-approval, and named clinical, legal, privacy, native, accessibility, and user acceptance owners |

## Raw Result - Subscription Trial Paywall

> The review uses the UI Review contract. The only supplied evidence is a text description, so the result is unverified consultation rather than visual, interaction, or App Store compliance validation.
>
> **Actual charge is visually subordinated - Blocking.** The headline says “7 days free,” `$4.99/month` is prominent, and the actual `$59.99 billed annually after trial` is tiny. People can interpret this as a monthly charge rather than one annual charge. The response also identified that `$59.99 / 12` rounds to about `$5.00`, so `$4.99/month` needs a real product basis.
>
> Recommended hierarchy: `7 days free, then $59.99/year`, with an accurate monthly equivalent kept subordinate. Final price, renewal period, trial eligibility, and localized content must come from the real StoreKit product.
>
> **Close button delayed five seconds - Blocking.** A person cannot immediately decline or leave when deciding whether to purchase. If the product permits deferral, close or back must be visible and operable from the first frame. If there is no free path, the exit result still needs to be transparent and predictable rather than delayed.
>
> **Restore path absent - Important, or Blocking when no other reachable restore path exists.** Existing subscribers may believe they need to purchase again. Provide a real restore path with restoring, restored, nothing-to-restore, failure, and retry states, then verify it in StoreKit sandbox or the project’s approved test environment.
>
> The result explicitly left actual size, contrast, hit areas, safe areas, close-button behavior, restore availability elsewhere, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, localization, real price, trial eligibility, renewal, entitlement, cancellation, pending, failure, purchase restoration, legal compliance, App Review, and user acceptance unverified.

## Raw Result - Blood-Pressure Insight

> The product cannot publish “You are at high risk” or a treatment plan from the supplied evidence. This is not a copy-polish issue: clinical judgment and treatment advice lack a responsible owner and source, so only the affected claim is blocked.
>
> Work that can continue includes a neutral information architecture, verified measurement facts, source and review-date locations, non-clinical states such as data insufficient, data stale, awaiting professional review, and error, plus Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, non-color meaning, long-content, and localization behavior.
>
> Work that cannot continue includes choosing thresholds or algorithms, diagnosing risk, recommending medication, dosage, treatment duration, lifestyle-treatment intensity, follow-up timing, or an emergency path. A generic “consult a doctor” disclaimer cannot substitute for professional review.
>
> The acceptance path requires a named clinical owner and legal entity; intended use, audience, and jurisdiction; approved sources, inputs, exclusions, uncertainty, wording, action and escalation rules; legal or regulatory and privacy review; interface-version sign-off; native device validation; accessibility and localization validation; representative-user comprehension; and final product-owner visual acceptance.

## Evidence Limits

These runs establish fresh-context method behavior for two realistic prompts. They do not establish that a specific paywall is visually present, that StoreKit behavior works, that a health conclusion is clinically valid, that any flow complies with law or App Review, or that users accept the experience.

The preserved FrameKeep HTML evidence remains separate. It supports only the simulated app-level permission and recovery path documented in `cases.json`, not native Photos permission or privacy compliance.
