# Interaction and Motion Method Record - 2026-07-31

## Scope

Issue #14 consolidates the existing product-authority, interaction, motion, platform-input, prototype, and evidence rules into one conditionally loaded method. The deterministic suite covers seven fixed cases across tool, content, and experimental products and all three bundled Apple UI skills.

## Maintained Behavior

- `$apple-ui-direction` defines the interaction contract and motion language, creates meaningful behavioral hypotheses only when direction is unresolved, and preserves confirmed choices.
- `$apple-platform-adaptation` translates the confirmed model to platform-specific touch, Pencil, keyboard, pointer, focus, command, window, and system-event behavior.
- `$apple-ui-review` assesses supplied behavior against confirmed intent, product-specific risks, interruption, reversal, recovery, Reduce Motion, and the evidence actually represented.
- Common Apple patterns and native controls remain platform evidence and risk-control tools rather than visual or product authority.
- Native interaction or motion quality cannot be inferred from HTML, static frames, timing tables, or successful compilation.

That evidence establishes expansion, interruption, reversal, final-intent handling, Pause, Finish, and the standard and Reduce Motion expressions for the named tool-product path on the recorded iPhone 15 Pro / iOS 17.5 Simulator environment.

It does not establish content-product motion quality, experimental-interaction learnability, production integration, a real-device result, broad accessibility completion, or user acceptance. The content and experimental cases are deterministic method coverage only.

## Acceptance Boundary

The maintained method can establish that the Skill requests and classifies the right artifacts and evidence. Final rhythm, feel, and aesthetic acceptance still require Rex to inspect the actual native experience. Any future experience-verification claim remains limited to the platform, environment, input, states, settings, and path that were exercised and preserved.
